Structured Trust Architecture

The 5 Layers of Structured Trust

Every dollar that flows through vvEntra is protected by five layers of structured trust. Identity verification. Escrow custody. Staged reveal. Clear dispute resolution. And for premium deals, a platform-backed guarantee.

The problem every idea marketplace has failed to solve.

Buying a business opportunity is fundamentally different from buying a physical product. Once intellectual property is revealed, it cannot be returned. This creates an unsolvable trust problem, until you engineer around it.

Architect's Dilemma
"If I show the buyer my opportunity, they will steal it without paying. I cannot show the substance until I am paid."
Creators fear intellectual property theft from curious browsers who never intended to fund execution.
Buyer's Dilemma
"If I pay before seeing the opportunity, the architect could deliver junk or something I already know. I cannot pay until I see the substance."
Investors and operators fear paying thousands of dollars for generic templates or superficial AI summaries.
The vvEntra Solution
"Neither party reveals or pays first. Both commit progressively through a staged reveal protected by escrow custody."
Exposure strictly matches commitment across 5 distinct tiers. Neutral banking escrow holds capital until verified inspection.

Trust is not one thing. It is five layers engineered together.

Each layer protects against a specific failure mode. Together they create an environment where bad actors are filtered at every step.

🪪

1. Identity & Reputation Verification

Every architect and buyer is a verified human with public stake. Government ID (Aadhaar / passport), penny-drop bank ownership, LinkedIn capacity, and a strict one-person-one-account policy.

🏦

2. Escrow Custody on Every Transaction

Money never moves directly from buyer to seller. Every dollar is held in regulated escrow before it reaches the architect. Releases only upon satisfaction during the 7-day inspection window.

🔒

3. Staged Reveal: Four Progressive Tiers

Buyers do not pay full price at once: Tier 0 (Free Preview) → Tier 1 (Verified Thesis) → Tier 2 ($100–$500 Deposit) → Tier 3 (NDA + 30% Partial Unlock) → Tier 4 (Remaining 70% Full Unlock).

⚖️

4. Clear Dispute Resolution (5-Day SLA)

Disputes are resolved within five business days by vvEntra moderation using documented criteria. Documented evidence required. Not for subjective buyer’s remorse.

🛡️

5. Platform Guarantee (Deals > $10k)

For transactions above $10,000, vvEntra itself guarantees payment within 48 hours. Architects receive guaranteed settlement; buyers receive verified premium standing. Up to $50,000 coverage cap.

📑

6. Minimum 90-Page Documentation Depth

Every listing undergoes manual moderation review. Must include a 30+ page business model, 25+ page execution workflows/SOPs, verified demand signals, financial models, and a 24-month roadmap.

Refunds are structured. Not subjective.

vvEntra uses documented eligibility criteria, applied consistently by moderation, so buyers are protected against misrepresentation and architects are protected against bad-faith reversals.

Eligible for Escrow Refund:

  • ✓ Documentation depth materially less than advertised (e.g., promised 110 pages, delivered 35)
  • ✓ Content is plagiarised, duplicate, or AI boilerplate
  • ✓ Architect made factually false claims about credentials or exit track record
  • ✓ Critical promised deliverables missing (e.g. financial model promised but omitted)
  • ✓ Opportunity fundamentally differs from Tier 0–2 previews

NOT Eligible for Refund (Subjective):

  • ✕ Buyer simply disagrees with the strategic market thesis
  • ✕ Buyer changes their mind after consuming the 90+ page playbook
  • ✕ Buyer claims they "already knew" the opportunity (unverifiable)
  • ✕ Buyer’s internal investment committee shifts corporate priorities
  • ✕ Failure to reach agreement on discretionary post-unlock advisory terms

Frequently Asked Questions

Key regulatory, legal, and settlement guidelines for institutional buyers and asset operators

For Institutional Buyers & Capital Allocators

Q: How is buyer capital protected during due diligence?

All buyer funds are deposited directly into neutral, regulated banking escrow accounts. The operator cannot access these funds during your 7-day inspection window. If deliverables materially deviate from verified specifications, funds are refunded in full under our documented SLA.

Q: What occurs during the technical diligence review session?

Upon 10% escrow deposit confirmation, you receive direct calendar booking access to the operating principal. Under a binding bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), you conduct a 45-minute technical audit examining architecture schematics, accounting records, live integrations, and supplier contracts.

Q: What are the specific criteria for receiving a 100% escrow refund?

Refunds are automatically approved if: (1) deliverable documentation is materially less than stated (fewer than 90 pages or missing operational frameworks), (2) advertised financial models or vendor rosters are omitted, or (3) the operator fails to respond to technical inquiries within the 48-hour SLA window.

Q: Can I purchase the intellectual property directly without ongoing operator involvement?

Yes. At closing, buyers select from three transaction structures: Asset Transfer Only (direct clean IP buyout), Strategic Advisory (retaining the operator for weekly oversight), or Full Operating Partnership (90/10 milestone-based execution partnership).

For Asset Operators & Sellers

Q: How are proprietary operational blueprints protected prior to payment?

Core operational IP—including source code repositories, vendor agreements, and financial cash-flow models—is never publicly exposed. Buyers only see sanitized executive abstracts until they complete identity verification, execute a mutual NDA, and place 10% cash deposit into regulated escrow.

Q: What is the mandatory 90-page documentation threshold?

vvEntra exclusively lists institutional-grade operational assets. Submissions must include complete procedural workflows (SOPs), technical data schemas, three-statement financial models, and risk mitigation registers. Abstract pitch decks without operational depth are systematically rejected.

Q: What fees does vvEntra charge asset operators?

Intake and technical audits are 100% free upfront. vvEntra retains a flat 10% facility commission strictly upon successful escrow clearance and buyer approval. Operating principals retain 90% of all gross deal proceeds.

Q: How and when are seller transaction payouts settled?

Payouts are cleared within 7 business days of buyer milestone approval or conclusion of the 7-day inspection window. Settlements are transferred directly to verified commercial bank accounts via FedWire, SWIFT, or domestic ACH rails.
